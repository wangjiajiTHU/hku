## PhD Admission 2027

The Structural Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (HKU Structural AI Lab) is recruiting **several full-time PhD students for entry in 2027**, supervised by Assistant Professor Jiaji Wang.

We welcome outstanding undergraduate and master's applicants from **civil engineering, engineering mechanics, computer science** and related fields. You are not expected to already have all of the skills below — we work at the boundary between structural engineering, AI and robotics, and applicants may come from any of those sides.

**Before you write, read some of the work.** The complete publication record is on the [HKU Scholars Hub](https://hub.hku.hk/cris/rp/rp03096), with a searchable version on the [Publications](publications.html) page and the current directions under [Research](research.html). An application that engages with a specific paper or research direction tells us far more than a generic statement of interest.

### Research Directions

**Composite and modular structures** — high-performance steel-concrete composite structures, modular structures and their inter-module connections, structural performance and design optimisation.

**Structural digital twins and intelligent design** — combining finite element analysis, machine learning and engineering data for structural condition identification, performance prediction, generative design and intelligent optimisation; digital twin models of real, complex structures under extreme loading; scientific AI algorithms for the spatio-temporal response of complex engineering structures.

**Physical AI and embodied intelligent robotics** — AI methods that fuse physical mechanisms with data-driven learning, together with multimodal perception, environment understanding, motion planning, autonomous decision-making and reinforcement learning for robots; applications of embodied intelligent robots in construction, fabrication, inspection and maintenance.

The specific topic is settled together, based on your background, your interests and the group's direction.

### Scholarships and Funding

Strong applicants are nominated for the **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)** and the **HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS)**. At HKU, HKPFS awardees also receive HKU-PS and matching university support, so the two packages come out broadly equivalent — roughly **HK$1.7 million (about USD 219,300) over four years**, comprising:

- a monthly PhD stipend
- tuition waiver for the normal period of study
- research and living cash awards
- an annual conference and research travel allowance
- guaranteed on-campus accommodation in the first year

The HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme provides about HK$440,000 in the first year and about HK$420,000 in each subsequent year.

Strong applicants who do not receive either award may still apply for the **HKU Postgraduate Scholarship (PGS)**, currently **HK$19,135 per month**, rising to **HK$19,655 per month** after confirmation of PhD candidature — about USD 119,360 over a four-year PhD, or about USD 58,880 for an MPhil.

We also welcome applications for **MPhil**, **self-funded PhD**, **part-time PhD** and **HKU-BICI joint PhD** places.

### Requirements

Applicants should hold a bachelor's degree or above in civil engineering, engineering mechanics, computer science or a related field before entry. We look for:

- a good academic record and solid disciplinary grounding
- enthusiasm, initiative and a sense of responsibility in research
- good English reading, writing and communication

Applicants holding a National Scholarship or first-class scholarship, ranking highly in their cohort, or with outstanding research experience will be prioritised for HKPFS and HKU-PS nomination. **Publications are not required to apply.**

### Deadlines

Entry is in **2027**, and the date to work back from is:

**Contact us by 10 October 2026.**

That is the one that matters. Nomination for the Presidential Scholarship and HKPFS is decided inside the group well before the university's formal deadline, so an email arriving in November is already too late to be put forward — however strong it is.

The formal university deadlines that follow are:

- **1 December 2026, 12:00 noon (HKT, GMT+8)** — HKPFS application to the Research Grants Council
- **1 December 2026** — full application to HKU via the Graduate School

## Postdoctoral Fellows

Postdoctoral positions are available for candidates with a PhD in structural engineering, computational mechanics, or scientific machine learning. Experience in one or more of the following is particularly welcome: physics-informed neural networks and neural operators, nonlinear constitutive modelling, large-scale structural testing, robotics, or digital twin development. We also support applications to the RGC Postdoctoral Fellowship Scheme and the HKU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship.

Email **cewang[at]hku.hk** with **"Postdoc Application"** in the subject line, attaching your CV, a short research statement and the contact details of two referees.

## 2027年香港大学结构智能课题组博士生招生

香港大学土木工程系结构智能课题组（HKU Structural AI Lab）现招收多个2027年入学的全日制博士研究生。欢迎来自土木大类、力学大类、计算机大类等相关专业的优秀本科生及硕士生申请。合作导师为汪家继助理教授。

### 一、研究方向

课题组围绕结构智能科学开展研究，主要包括：

**组合结构与模块化结构**：高性能钢-混凝土组合结构、模块化结构及其连接节点、结构性能与设计优化。

**结构数字孪生与智能设计**：融合有限元、机器学习和工程数据，开展结构状态识别、性能预测、生成式设计与智能优化。开发实际复杂工程在极端荷载下的数字孪生模型。开发科学智能算法求解复杂工程结构时空响应。

**物理人工智能与具身智能机器人**：研究融合物理机理与数据驱动的人工智能方法，以及机器人的多模态感知、环境理解、运动规划、自主决策和强化学习算法，探索具身智能机器人在施工、建造、巡检、运维等复杂场景中的应用。

具体研究课题将根据申请人的专业背景、研究兴趣和课题组发展方向共同确定。欢迎具有土木大类、力学大类或计算机大类的同学申请，不要求同时掌握上述所有技能。

### 二、奖学金待遇

优秀申请人将获重点支持申请：香港博士研究生奖学金计划（HKPFS）和香港大学校长博士生奖学金（HKU-PS）。在香港大学，HKPFS获奖者同时可获得HKU-PS及学校配套资助，因此两项奖学金的整体资助待遇基本一致。

按照香港大学现行标准，HKPFS和HKU-PS博士生的综合资助总额约170万港币，主要包括：每月博士生奖学金；正常修读年限内学费豁免；研究及生活现金奖励；每年会议和科研差旅资助；首年校内住宿保障。香港大学校长奖学金第一年资助约44万港币，之后每年约42万港币，四年合计约170万港币。

未获得上述两项入学奖学金的优秀申请人，也可根据学校规定申请香港大学研究生奖学金（PGS）。目前PGS基础标准为每月19,135港币，通过博士候选资格确认后为每月19,655港币。

我们也欢迎研究型硕士生MPhil、自费博士生、part-time博士生、HKU-BICI合作博士生等项目的申请。

### 三、申请条件

申请人应在入学前取得土木大类、力学大类或计算机大类的本科或以上学位。我们期待申请人：具有良好的学习成绩和专业基础；对科研具有热情、主动性和责任感；具有良好的英文阅读、写作与交流能力。

获得国家奖学金、一等奖学金、专业排名靠前或具有突出科研经历的申请人，将获重点支持申请HKPFS及HKU-PS。**没有发表论文也可以申请。**
